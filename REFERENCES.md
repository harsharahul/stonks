# References

Prior work and documentation that informed the design of Stonks.

- Xiao, Y., Sun, E., Luo, D., Wang, W. "TradingAgents: Multi-Agents LLM Financial Trading Framework." arXiv:2412.20138, 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20138. The analyst, researcher, trader, and risk-management role structure that the AI Trading Desk runs.
- Hutto, C. J., Gilbert, E. "VADER: A Parsimonious Rule-based Model for Sentiment Analysis of Social Media Text." ICWSM, 2014. The sentiment scorer applied to headlines and Reddit posts.
- Wilder, J. W. "New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems." Trend Research, 1978. The Relative Strength Index shown on stock charts.
- Appel, G. "Technical Analysis: Power Tools for Active Investors." FT Prentice Hall, 2005. The MACD indicator.
- Bollinger, J. "Bollinger on Bollinger Bands." McGraw-Hill, 2001. The volatility bands on price charts.
- Nison, S. "Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques." New York Institute of Finance, 1991. The candlestick pattern definitions used by the pattern detector.
- U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. "Accessing EDGAR Data." https://www.sec.gov/os/accessing-edgar-data. Fair-access requirements for automated clients, including the declared User-Agent contact.
- U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. "Form 13F." https://www.sec.gov/divisions/investment/13ffaq.htm. Quarterly institutional holdings behind the fund-tracking signal plugin.
- U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. "Forms 3, 4, and 5." https://www.sec.gov/about/forms/form4.pdf. Insider transaction reporting referenced by the insider-activity signal type.
- Alpaca Markets. "Trading API documentation." https://docs.alpaca.markets/. Order types, bracket orders, and paper-trading semantics used by the broker layer.
- OpenID Foundation. "OpenID Connect Core 1.0." https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-core-1_0.html. Identity flow, with PKCE per RFC 7636 for the public client.
- Preston-Werner, T. "Keep a Changelog." https://keepachangelog.com/. The changelog format.
